## Replacement narrative for Chapter I: scope and delimitation

LearnSync supports the Bachelor of Science in Entrepreneurship program of Governor Mariano E. Villafuerte Community College (GMVCC) through an integrated learning management system, assessment record, approved syllabus outline, and AI academic assistant. Administrators manage accounts and the academic structure: school year and semester, subjects, shared sections, advisers, rosters, and faculty assignments. Students may request membership in a section using its code; the administrator reviews the request. An approved roster normally grants access to the subjects assigned to that section. An irregular student retains one home section per term while an administrator may persistently exempt a subject or include one taught in another section. The chosen teaching section determines the faculty roster and assessment audience.

Faculty members work from their assigned subjects. They may import a DOCX or text based PDF syllabus, correct the extracted course information, outcomes, chapters, optional lesson subsections, topics, references, materials, assessments, and grading rules, and approve a version for use. They publish reviewed learning content, quizzes, and activities, with section specific visibility and due dates where needed. Online quizzes may be scored automatically after faculty review. Activities, daily attendance, participation, score-only face-to-face quizzes, and other work requiring judgment are recorded or evaluated by faculty. The roster gradebook and calculated preview use the approved syllabus rule; faculty review and explicitly publish grades before students can view them.

The AI assistant and quiz draft generation remain supporting functions based on teacher provided learning materials. Faculty review generated quizzes before publication. Scanned PDF syllabi require manual correction, and student promotion and broader administrative processes are outside this faculty focused revision. This revision does not claim automated scoring of asynchronous activities, predictive analytics, or integration with external learning platforms.

## Replacement narrative for Chapter III: user design and data flow

The revised design separates academic setup from instructional delivery. The administrator creates a term, subjects, and shared sections, assigns advisers and rosters, and connects a faculty member and subject to one or more sections. A student may submit a section join request, and approval updates the shared roster and offering enrollments. The faculty dashboard retrieves only assigned active offerings. Faculty members import or create a syllabus draft, resolve extraction warnings, review grading rules, and approve a version. Several lesson modules may belong to each approved chapter. Activities and quizzes have one course, chapter, lesson subsection, or topic placement, while teaching-section audience is configured separately. Section overrides determine item visibility and due dates. Assessment records feed a calculated preview, and only an explicit faculty publication creates a student visible grade snapshot. The revised [context and faculty DFD](docs/diagrams/DFD.md) replace the teacher owned class creation and direct student enrollment paths shown in the proposal.

The [updated ERD](docs/diagrams/ERD.md) preserves the proposal's account, teacher, student, content, assessment, and AI entities while adding terms, subject catalog, shared roster, assignment links, syllabus versions, and grade publications. The physical database keeps legacy `class`, `section`, `enrollment`, uploads, and score identifiers. Records without a reliable term or shared section mapping enter an administrator review queue before they become active offerings.
